Made for Joyce

For parents

Brain Breaks are short learning pauses instead of ads. At the end of every level, whether Joyce finishes it or starts it over, she or Miriam answers a few questions: math, word problems, English, Russian, and Hebrew words, and the weekly Torah portion. Questions get a little harder as the game level goes up. Each child has her own level, saved on this iPad. They can switch players on the break screen.

The 💬 bubble lets Joyce chat with Sofie, her Grok Bot. It asks for the family code once on this iPad, and only Sofie can answer.
